# Corner Pharmacy — website for a neighbourhood pharmacy

**Mode:** static (pages only, no backend)

Corner Pharmacy is an independent pharmacy. They want a website with their services, opening hours, where to find
them, and how prescription collection works.

## Pages

1. **Home** — what the pharmacy offers, opening hours and the address.
2. **Services** — prescriptions, flu vaccinations, blood pressure checks, travel health, advice on minor illness.
3. **Prescriptions** — how collection works: send the prescription, we text you when it is ready, collect within
   seven days.
4. **Contact** — +44 20 7946 0123, 2 Mill Street, Bristol. Mon–Sat 08:30–19:00, Sun 10:00–16:00.
