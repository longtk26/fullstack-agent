# Ledgerline — website for an accounting practice

**Mode:** static (pages only, no backend)

Ledgerline is an accounting practice for freelancers and small companies. They want a website with their
services, fixed monthly prices, how onboarding works and how to get in touch.

## Pages

1. **Home** — who they help, the services and "Book an intro call".
2. **Services** — bookkeeping, annual accounts, tax returns, payroll for up to ten people.
3. **Pricing** — freelancer €49 / month, small company €149 / month, payroll €8 per person.
4. **How it works** — intro call, upload last year's records, monthly check-in.
