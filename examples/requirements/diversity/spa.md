# Still Water — website for a day spa

**Mode:** static (pages only, no backend)

Still Water is a small day spa in a converted boathouse by a lake. They want a website that shows their treatments,
prices and opening hours and lets people book by phone or e-mail.

## Pages

1. **Home** — what the spa is, the four treatments, the opening hours and a "Book a treatment" link to Contact.
2. **Treatments** — massage (60 min, €85), facial (45 min, €70), sauna and lake swim (90 min, €40), couples
   package (2 h, €190).
3. **About** — the boathouse, opened in 2018 by Mira and Jonas, both trained therapists.
4. **Contact** — book@stillwater.spa, +49 30 1234 5678, Seeweg 4, Potsdam. Open Tue–Sun 10:00–20:00.
