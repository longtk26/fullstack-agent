# Second Shot — website for a specialty coffee shop

**Mode:** static (pages only, no backend)

Second Shot is a specialty coffee shop that roasts its own beans. They want a website with the drinks, the beans
they sell, the opening hours and where to find them.

## Pages

1. **Home** — what makes the coffee special, the opening hours and the address.
2. **Menu** — espresso €2.50, flat white €3.60, filter €3.20, cold brew €4; pastries from a local bakery.
3. **Beans** — three single origins this month (Ethiopia Guji, Colombia Huila, Kenya Nyeri), 250 g for €14.
4. **Visit** — 9 Harbour Road, Copenhagen. Mon–Fri 07:30–17:00, Sat–Sun 09:00–16:00. hello@secondshot.coffee.
