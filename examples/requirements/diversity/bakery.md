# Crumb & Co — website for a sourdough bakery

**Mode:** static (pages only, no backend)

Crumb & Co is a small bakery that bakes sourdough and pastries every morning. They want a website with the menu,
the opening hours, their story and how to order a cake.

## Pages

1. **Home** — what they bake, today's opening hours, and "Order a cake" linking to Contact.
2. **Menu** — breads (country loaf €6, rye €5.50, seeded €6.50), pastries (croissant €2.80, cinnamon bun €3.20),
   cakes to order (from €35).
3. **Our story** — started in 2015 in a garage oven; now a shop with a wood-fired oven.
4. **Contact** — orders@crumbandco.com, +33 1 23 45 67 89, 14 rue des Boulangers, Lyon. Tue–Sun 07:00–14:00.
