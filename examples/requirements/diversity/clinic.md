# Riverside Family Clinic — website for a family doctor's practice

**Mode:** static (pages only, no backend)

Riverside is a family practice with three doctors and a nurse. They want a website that tells patients what they
treat, who the doctors are, the opening hours and how to make an appointment.

## Pages

1. **Home** — who the practice is for, the services, the opening hours and "Call to make an appointment".
2. **Services** — check-ups, vaccinations, children's health, chronic care, travel advice.
3. **Our team** — Dr. Amara Okafor, Dr. Pieter de Vries, Dr. Hana Sato, nurse Tom Becker.
4. **Patient information** — what to bring to a first visit; repeat prescriptions by phone; out-of-hours number.
5. **Contact** — +31 20 555 0101, Rivierweg 8, Amsterdam. Mon–Fri 08:00–17:30.
