# Kettle & Leaf — tea shop with online orders

**Mode:** dynamic (frontend and backend)

Kettle & Leaf sells loose-leaf tea and teaware from a small shop. Customers order online for pick-up or delivery;
the shop staff see new orders and mark them packed and sent.

## Screens

1. **Home** — what the shop sells, this month's tea and "Order for pick-up".
2. **Teas** — every tea with its origin, a short taste note and the price per 50 g; filter by green, black, oolong
   and herbal.
3. **Tea detail** — the tea, how to brew it, and add to the order.
4. **Your order** — the teas in the order, pick-up or delivery, and checkout with name, phone and address.
5. **Orders** (staff) — new, packed and sent orders with their items and totals.
