# Ironworks — website for a strength gym

**Mode:** static (pages only, no backend)

Ironworks is a strength and conditioning gym in an old foundry. They want a website that shows the classes, the
coaches and the membership prices, and how to book a free first session.

## Pages

1. **Home** — what the gym is, the class types, and a "Book a free session" link to Contact.
2. **Classes** — barbell basics (Mon/Wed 18:00), conditioning (Tue/Thu 07:00), open gym (daily 06:00–22:00).
3. **Coaches** — Dana (powerlifting), Kofi (conditioning), Lena (mobility).
4. **Membership** — monthly €59, ten-session card €120, students €39.
5. **Contact** — hello@ironworks.gym, +44 161 496 0000, Foundry Lane 12, Manchester.
