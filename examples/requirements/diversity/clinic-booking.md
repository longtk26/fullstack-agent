# Riverside Family Clinic — appointment booking app

**Mode:** dynamic (frontend and backend)

Riverside is a family practice with three doctors. Patients book, move and cancel appointments online; the front
desk sees the day's list and marks patients as arrived.

## Screens

1. **Home** — the practice in one line, the opening hours and "Book an appointment".
2. **Book** — choose a doctor, a day and a free time, then give name, phone and the reason for the visit.
3. **My appointments** — a patient's upcoming appointments with move and cancel.
4. **Today** (front desk) — every appointment of the day by time, with doctor, patient and status (booked, arrived,
   done, cancelled).
5. **Doctors** — Dr. Amara Okafor, Dr. Pieter de Vries, Dr. Hana Sato, each with the days they work.
