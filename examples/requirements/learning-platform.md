# Learnly — an online course platform

**Mode:** dynamic (web app with its own backend)
**Matches:** `specs/learning-platform.json` + `app-outlines/learning-platform.json` (drafted by Gemini on 2026-11-06, Story 23.0; the fake engine replays it)

An online course platform for a small training company. Managers create courses with a title, a cover image, a
level (beginner, intermediate, advanced), a category, a price, a short summary and a status (draft or published).
Each course has lessons in order, each with a title, a duration in minutes and a video link. Learners enrol in a
published course, see their enrolments with progress in percent, and mark lessons as done.

Managers follow the business on a dashboard: enrolments this month, revenue per course, and the most popular
categories. They publish or unpublish courses and see every learner's enrolments.

Start with six courses across Design, Programming and Marketing, a few lessons each.
