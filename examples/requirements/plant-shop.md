# Leafy Corner — a small online plant shop

**Mode:** dynamic (web app with its own backend)
**Matches:** `specs/plant-shop.json` + `app-outlines/plant-shop.json` (drafted by Gemini on 2026-11-06, Story 23.0; the fake engine replays it)

A small online plant shop. Plants have a name, a photo, a price, a size (small, medium, large), a light need (low,
medium, bright), whether they are pet safe, a stock count and a short care note. Plants belong to collections
(Easy care, Pet friendly, Statement plants, Hanging plants).

Shoppers browse the plants by collection, filter by light and size, open a plant to read its care note, add it to
their cart and pay. They see their past orders and each order's status (paid, packed, shipped, delivered). The
owner adds plants, updates stock and moves orders along.

Start with the four collections and about ten plants.
