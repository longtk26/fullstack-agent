# Northwind Studio — website for a two-person design studio

**Mode:** static (pages only, no backend)
**Matches:** `frontend-specs/static-landing.json`

Northwind is a small brand and web design studio. They want a simple website that explains what they do, shows
their work and makes it easy to get in touch. They will host it on any static host; there is nothing to log in
to and no data to store.

## Pages

1. **Home** — headline "Design that sounds like you", subline "Brand and web design for independent
   businesses.", an image of the studio desk and a "See our work" button. Then the three services as cards, a
   client quote, and a closing banner "Have a project?" with a "Get in touch" button.
2. **Services** — the services again, two packages side by side (Starter £1,500: logo, palette, one-page site;
   Studio £4,000: full identity, five-page site, print kit) and two questions with answers ("How long does a
   project take?" — four to eight weeks; "Do you work remotely?" — yes, with clients anywhere).
3. **Work** — a gallery of two projects, Bakehouse (packaging) and Harbour Books (shop sign).
4. **About** — one paragraph ("Northwind is a two-person studio. We have helped forty small businesses find
   their voice since 2019.") and two numbers: 40+ clients, 6 years.
5. **Contact** — e-mail hello@northwind.studio and phone +44 20 7946 0000.

Navigation: Services, Work, About, Contact. Footer: "© Northwind Studio", a link to their Instagram and to
Contact.

## Services (for the cards)

- Brand identity — logo, palette, type and a short guide.
- Websites — fast, accessible sites you can edit yourself.
- Print — menus, packaging, signage.

## Look and feel

Editorial: warm off-white background, near-black text, a burnt-orange accent (#c2410c), serif headings, small
corner radius, generous spacing. Light mode only.

## Done when

The five pages are built as a static site, every link works, and the content above appears exactly as written.
