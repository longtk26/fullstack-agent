# StudioTube

A video library for a company's training and product videos. Videos belong to channels (Engineering, Sales, Design,
Onboarding). Each video has a title, a description, a thumbnail, a duration, a view count, a publish date and a
category. People browse videos by channel or category, search by title, open a video page with its description and
the other videos of that channel, and add videos to their own playlists.

Channels have a name, a banner image, a short description and a subscriber count. Creators upload videos to their
channels and edit them; viewers can only watch and make playlists.

Start with the four channels above and a handful of videos in each.
