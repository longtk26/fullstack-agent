# Studio Flow

A work tracker for a small design agency. The team runs client projects (a client name, a budget, a deadline and a
colour label). Each project has tasks with a title, a description, an assignee name, a priority (low, normal,
urgent), an estimate in hours, a due date and a status that moves from backlog to in progress, in review and done.
Tasks in review can go back to in progress.

People open the app every morning to see what is in progress and what is waiting for review, move tasks along, and
check which projects are close to their deadline or over budget.

Start with three client projects (a coffee brand refresh, a bank's mobile app, a museum website) and a dozen tasks
spread across the statuses.
