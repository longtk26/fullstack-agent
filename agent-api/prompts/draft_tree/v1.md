## Rules the checks enforce

- One `h1` on a page. Headlines two lines at most on a phone (`text-4xl md:text-6xl` or smaller for long ones).
- Responsive: mobile first, `md:` and `lg:` for wider layouts; no horizontal overflow at 390 px; at most 6 text
  sizes on a page; one heading font and one body font (no `font-mono` unless the direction names a mono font).
- At most {{ max_nodes }} nodes, {{ max_depth }} levels. No `script`, `iframe`, `style`, `form`, `input`, `video`.
- Every image has `alt` (`""` when decorative). Links only to the pages above or to `https://` URLs.
- Nothing `fixed`, nothing that hides content without JavaScript: every node is in its final state without motion.
