# Story 26.2 — Frozen expectations

**Epic:** [26 — Main business workflows](epic26_business_workflows.md) · **Requirements:** FR-40 · **Depends on:** 26.1

## User story

As a business owner, I want what my app must do at every workflow step written down before it is built, so it is
judged against my business and not against whatever was generated.

## Context

The lab's `expect` stage (`expect_prompt`, `EXPECT_SCHEMA`): per path (main and each branch) and step: `allowed`,
`refused` (with `other:<actor>` for another person of the same role), `record`, `statusAfter`, `visibleTo`,
`hiddenFrom`, `effects` (`decrease` / `increase` / `set` / `created`), `refusedBefore` (never on the step that creates
the record), and the outcome each actor should see. Business words only.

## Steps

1. **Expectations call** (prompt `expect_workflows@v1`) right after discovery, through the provider; schema checked in
   code; stored as `spec_drafts.expectations` and packaged as `expected.json`. They are never rewritten after the draft
   (Epic 26 D2); a re-draft makes new ones.
2. **Readable form** for the review and the Business tab: one sentence per expectation, generated by code from the
   JSON (the lab's `expectation_texts`).
3. Tests: schema, `other:` actors, no `refusedBefore` on a creating step (code drops it with a note), the sentences.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every core workflow path has expectations for every step; they exist before the generation starts.
- [ ] A generation reads the expectations of its draft unchanged.

## Verify

`make lint`, `make test`.
